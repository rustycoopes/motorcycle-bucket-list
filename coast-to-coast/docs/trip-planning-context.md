# Coast-to-Coast Motorcycle Trip — Planning Context

**Rider:** Russ, based in Ridgefield, CT
**Trip type:** Two ONE-WAY rides, Ridgefield CT → Pacific, in two different years
**Status:** Rough itinerary. Not booked.

## Structure

Both routes run one way, westbound, from Ridgefield CT to the Pacific. No return leg — the bike is shipped home and the rider flies back. Two separate trips in different years, deliberately different lengths: the shorter one while still working, the longer one after retiring.

## Constraints and preferences

- Maximum 6 hours riding per day
- 180-mile fuel range. Auxiliary fuel carried as an expandable/removable pack, used only on flagged backcountry legs — not carried throughout.
- Hotels preferred; lightweight bivy as the exception, not the plan
- Verizon — known dead zones flagged per phase
- Priorities: twisty scenic roads over highway, historic sites (pioneering and Civil War), Native American heritage, natural wonders, city nightlife and culture, famous motorcycle museums and shops
- **Avoid (already visited):** Boston, New York, Seattle, Austin TX, the Outer Banks

## Fuel and cell-signal findings

Researched and sourced, not assumed.

- Nothing on either route exceeds a 180-mile gap between stations.
- **Chaco Canyon (S3):** zero services *and* confirmed zero cell reception, all carriers. The gas can is mandatory here.
- **Beartooth Highway (N4):** ~68 mi with one seasonal, limited-hours stop midway. Assume no signal.
- **Going-to-the-Sun Road (N5):** confirmed Verizon dead zone between The Loop and Logan Pass, 20–30 minutes.
- **Yellowstone (N4):** effectively one large dead zone, with pockets at Mammoth, Old Faithful, Lake Village, Grant Village and the entrances. Jackson and the Tetons have good signal.
- **Blue Ridge Parkway / Skyline Drive (S1):** inconsistent; false bars are common — the phone shows signal but nothing sends.
- **MT-83 (N5):** confirmed 10–15 minute gaps. Verizon is the best carrier in Montana at roughly 85% of major routes.
- The real risk in the rural West is station **hours**, not distance. Working rule: refuel at half a tank, not at the 180-mile mark.
