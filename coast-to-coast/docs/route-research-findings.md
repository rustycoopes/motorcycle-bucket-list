# Rider-Sourced Route Research

Research pass done September 2026 against the AdventureBikeRider coast-to-coast guide, Rider Magazine's "50 Best Motorcycle Roads in America," rider forums and route guides. Everything below is sourced rather than assumed.

## Iconic roads added, by phase

**S1 — Connecticut to Appalachia**
- Tail of the Dragon / US-129 at Deals Gap (11 mi, 318 curves) — the most famous motorcycle road in America. Heavily policed and packed at weekends; ride it midweek and early.
- Moonshiner 28 / NC-28 (81 mi) — the quieter road out of Deals Gap
- Back of the Dragon / VA-16, Marion→Tazewell (32 mi, 400+ curves)
- Georgia Triangle / GA-60, GA-180, US-129 (35 mi) — optional loop around Blood Mountain

**S2 — South & Ozarks**
- Natchez Trace Parkway (444 mi) — begins in Nashville. No commercial traffic, no billboards.
- Talimena National Scenic Byway / AR-88 → OK-1 (54 mi)
- Jasper Disaster Loop / AR-7, AR-43, AR-74 (56 mi) — crosses the Buffalo National River twice
- Pig Trail Scenic Byway / AR-23 (20 mi)

**S3 — Southwest**
- Enchanted Circle Scenic Byway (84 mi) — loops from Taos, which is already a stop, so near-free
- Coronado Trail / US-191 (123 mi) — eastern Arizona; a real detour but consistently top-rated

**S3B — Colorado & Utah (optional)**
- Million Dollar Highway / US-550, Ouray↔Silverton (25 mi)
- San Juan Skyway (236 mi full loop)
- Utah Scenic Byway 12 (123 mi)
- UT-128 Upper Colorado River Scenic Byway
- Moki Dugway / UT-261 — gravel switchbacks, on the alternate southern line via Monument Valley

**N1 / N2 — New England and the Great Lakes**
- Kancamagus Highway / NH-112 (35 mi)
- Vermont Route 100 (217 mi)
- Mount Washington Auto Road, Acadia Park Loop Road
- Upper Delaware Scenic Byway / NY-97, the "Hawk's Nest" (70 mi)
- Tunnel of Trees / M-119 (20 mi), Copper Country Trail (47 mi)

**N3 — Great Lakes to the Black Hills**
- Iron Mountain Road / US-16A (17 mi) — 314 curves, 14 switchbacks, three pigtail bridges
- Needles Highway — pairs with Iron Mountain as a loop
- Minnesota North Shore Scenic Drive

**N4 — Yellowstone & the Tetons**
- Beartooth Highway / US-212 (69 mi) — "the most scenic highway in America"
- Chief Joseph Scenic Byway — the fallback when Beartooth is closed for snow

**N5 / N6 — Rockies to the Pacific**
- Going-to-the-Sun Road (50 mi)
- North Cascades Scenic Highway / WA-20 (140 mi) — the one-way restructure fixed an earlier problem here: approached from the east, so Seattle stays off the route entirely
- West Cascades Scenic Byway (220 mi), Mount Hood Scenic Byway (105 mi), Cascade Lakes Byway, Crater Lake Rim Drive
- Historic Columbia River Highway

## Resolved: the Colorado & Utah gap → optional Phase S3B

The one genuine omission the research turned up. The original plan ran Chaco Canyon straight to the Grand Canyon, skipping both the Colorado San Juans and southern Utah — between them arguably the best motorcycling terrain in the country, and both on the AdventureBikeRider route.

Now added as **Phase S3B**: 8 days / 940 mi, net **+7 days / +640 mi** since it replaces the direct Chaco→Grand Canyon run.

Chaco/Farmington → Durango → Silverton → Ouray → Telluride → Mesa Verde → Moab → Capitol Reef → Byway 12 → Bryce → Page → Grand Canyon.

Mesa Verde is the quiet win here: UNESCO Ancestral Puebloan cliff dwellings, the natural companion to Chaco for the Native American thread running through the trip.

Flagged on the phase: this is the most demanding riding on either route. Red Mountain Pass has no guardrails, sheer drops and heavy summer RV traffic; days are spent above 9,000 ft; and the season is tight — the pass holds snow into late May and gets early storms from October, which makes S3B the main constraint on when the South Route can run at all.

Not included: Mount Blue Sky Scenic Byway and Pikes Peak. Both are long detours north toward Denver, off any sensible line.

## Motorcycle museums and shops

- **S1** Wheels Through Time, Maggie Valley NC — 5 mi off the Blue Ridge Parkway. 350+ rare American machines. Near-zero detour.
- **S2** Barber Vintage Motorsports, Birmingham AL — the largest motorcycle collection in the world, but ~200 mi south of Nashville. Optional.
- **S3** Buddy Stubbs, Phoenix AZ — free, 130+ machines. **Saturdays only, 11am–5pm** — time the arrival.
- **S4** ARCH Motorcycle, Hawthorne CA — Keanu Reeves' boutique manufacturer. A working factory, not a public museum; check their site for visit policy. Plus Solvang Vintage Motorcycle Museum, right on the LA–Big Sur coastal line.
- **N3** Sturgis Motorcycle Museum & Hall of Fame — ~30 mi from Rapid City, essentially on the way.
- **N2** Harley-Davidson Museum, Milwaukee and the AMA Motorcycle Hall of Fame, Pickerington OH — both only reachable if N2 routes south of the lakes rather than through the Adirondacks.

## Deliberately not included

- Twisted Sisters, Texas Hill Country — the southern return leg that would have reached it disappeared when the trips became one-way
- US-50 "Loneliest Road," Nevada — 160 mi with no fuel, and neither route crosses central Nevada
- Trans-America Trail and the Continental Divide Route — largely unpaved, wrong tool for this trip
- Great River Road, Door County — good roads, wrong geography
